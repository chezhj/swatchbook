What changes. One rule in _components.scss: .bottle drops its fixed width: 100px; height: 136px; for width: 100%; aspect-ratio: 100 / 136;. The columns are already flex: 1 in .comparecols, so they keep splitting the row evenly — the bottles simply grow to fill their half. Adding a max-width (e.g. 220 px) keeps them from becoming enormous on wide desktop screens while staying side-by-side on mobile.
